Neon Harbor is a fictional harbor city built for the Make GTA 6 bounty on make.fun. Everything in it is original: the city, the characters, the story, the art and the sound. It runs entirely offline from these static files — no network request, no library. Your cash and mission progress save in this browser (localStorage).
